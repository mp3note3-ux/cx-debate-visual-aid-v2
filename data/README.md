# Data notes

These are published aggregate tables and model estimates, not individual-level patient records.

## kdca_2025_2026_comparison.csv
Transcribed year-on-year aggregate values from the KDCA 2026 heat-illness surveillance results.
Primary source: https://www.kdca.go.kr/bbs/kdca/42/310219/download.do

Important: the surveillance system uses participating emergency departments (about 520 in 2026), not all hospitals or a census of all heat illnesses. The reported heat-illness total includes suspected heat-related deaths.

## geohealth_published_odds_ratios.csv
Published adjusted odds ratios and 95% confidence intervals from:
Kim et al. (2026), “Compound Heatwaves and Health Inequality: A Nationwide Study on Emergency Admissions and Socioeconomic Vulnerability in South Korea,” GeoHealth, 10(10), e2026GH001920.
https://doi.org/10.1029/2026GH001920

These are not raw NHIS admission records. The underlying data are subject to NHIS approval. Nighttime-only heatwave results are not statistically significant; compound day/night heatwave results are statistically significant.
